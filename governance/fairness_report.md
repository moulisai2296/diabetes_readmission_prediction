# Fairness Audit — 30-Day Readmission Model

Fairlearn audit on the held-out **test split** (14,859 encounters, 11.3% positive), at the operating threshold 0.1. Generated by `src/governance/fairness_audit.py`.

**Overall:** selection rate 0.472, recall 0.700, FPR 0.443, precision 0.168.

Recall is the fairness metric we weight most: a missed readmission (low recall) is the harm the system exists to prevent, so a recall gap means one group is protected less than another.
